Basics

Units apply everywhere a value appears. Readouts and rings update at the refresh interval, weather every 30 minutes, and everything when you come back to the face from another app.

Map Color default: Cyan

Color of the highlighted contour lines on the map.

Options: Amber, Blue, Cyan, Green, Light Gray, Red, Teal, White, Yellow

Map in Always-On default: On

Shows the map, dimmed, behind the time while the watch is in always-on mode. It uses about 1.5% of the screen's brightness budget and drifts 1 pixel a minute to spread screen wear. Turn it off for the time only. Always-on itself is turned on or off in the watch's display settings.

Options: On, Off

Time Color default: White

Color of the big time.

Options: Amber, Blue, Cyan, Green, Light Gray, Mint Green, Red, Teal, White, Yellow

Time Format default: Watch Setting

Used for the time, sunrise and sunset, and sleep and wake times. Watch Setting follows the format set on the watch.

Options: 12-hour, 24-hour, Watch Setting

AM / PM Label (12-hour time only) default: On

Shows AM or PM next to the time. Only appears when the time is in 12-hour format.

Options: On, Off

AM / PM Color default: Light Gray

Color of the AM/PM label next to the time.

Options: Amber, Blue, Cyan, Green, Light Gray, Mint Green, Red, Teal, White, Yellow

Ghost Dots (dim unlit LEDs behind the time; off saves a little power) default: On

Shows each digit's unlit dots dimly behind the time, like a real LED panel. Off saves a little power.

Options: On, Off

Distance Units default: Watch Setting

Used for distance, weekly run and bike distance, wind and altitude. Watch Setting follows the units set on the watch.

Options: Kilometers, Miles, Watch Setting

Temperature Units default: Watch Setting

Used for weather, feels-like, daily high/low and skin temperature. Watch Setting follows the units set on the watch.

Options: Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), Watch Setting

Data Refresh Interval default: Every 5 Minutes (Default)

How often the readouts and rings update. Longer saves battery.

Options: Every Minute, Every 5 Minutes (Default), Every 15 Minutes, Every 30 Minutes, Every Hour

Animations

Animations run for about 10 seconds after each wrist raise and increase battery draw. Turn Animations off to stop all of them.

Animations default: On

Turns all the animations below on or off at once. Off saves battery.

Options: On, Off

Blinking Colon default: On

The colon between the hours and minutes changes once a second while the screen is awake.

Options: On, Off

Tip: the colon changes exactly once a second, so you can count seconds by it.

Colon Blink Style default: On/off in place

Shift moves the colon one dot right and back. On/off turns it off and on in place. Either way it changes every second.

Options: Shift left/right, On/off in place

Ring Sweep (rings glide to a new value) default: On

When a ring's value changes, the ring sweeps smoothly to it instead of jumping.

Options: On, Off

Text Shortening

Long counts can switch to K (thousands) to keep the readouts short.

Shorten Calories default: Full up to 9,999, then 12K

Counts below this size show in full. Bigger counts switch to K (thousands), so 12K means 12,000.

Options: Full up to 999, then 1.2K, Full up to 9,999, then 12K, Full up to 99,999, then 123K

Shorten Steps default: Full up to 9,999, then 12K

Counts below this size show in full. Bigger counts switch to K (thousands), so 12K means 12,000.

Options: Full up to 999, then 1.2K, Full up to 9,999, then 12K, Full up to 99,999, then 123K
